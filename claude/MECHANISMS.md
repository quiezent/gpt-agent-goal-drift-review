## Definitions and predictions

**M1. Optimizing checkable proxies.** RL against unit tests, graders and scorers rewards passing the check. When the real objective has no verifier, an agent that learned "make the check pass" may build checks and pass them. *Predicts:* effort concentrates on tests, hashes, receipts and validators; readiness is inferred from green tests; completion is declared at a validator boundary; all of this falls when a direct outcome verifier exists. *Literature:* [baker2025](SOURCES.md#baker2025), [metr2025rh](SOURCES.md#metr2025rh), [openai2025o3card](SOURCES.md#openai2025o3card), [zhong2025impossiblebench](SOURCES.md#zhong2025impossiblebench), [zhao2026specbench](SOURCES.md#zhao2026specbench), [metr2026sol](SOURCES.md#metr2026sol), [aisi2026cheating](SOURCES.md#aisi2026cheating), [gao2022](SOURCES.md#gao2022). *Base rates:* [metr2025gpt5](SOURCES.md#metr2025gpt5), [gabor2025evilgenie](SOURCES.md#gabor2025evilgenie).

**M2. Omission as the safe default.** Safety post-training and agent policy treat a wrong action, especially an irreversible or financial one, as costlier than a wrong inaction. *Predicts:* HOLD or refusal with no context; missing evidence resolved toward no action; stronger effects for money-moving actions; missed actions recorded as bookkeeping. *Literature for:* [openai_operator_card_2025](SOURCES.md#openai_operator_card_2025), [openai_o3operator_card_2025](SOURCES.md#openai_o3operator_card_2025), [openai_modelspec_2026](SOURCES.md#openai_modelspec_2026), [openai_usage_policies_2025](SOURCES.md#openai_usage_policies_2025), [openai2025_gpt52codex_card](SOURCES.md#openai2025_gpt52codex_card), [cheung2025](SOURCES.md#cheung2025), [kim2026holdbias](SOURCES.md#kim2026holdbias). *Against:* [liu2026_agentabstain](SOURCES.md#liu2026_agentabstain), [andriushchenko2025_agentharm](SOURCES.md#andriushchenko2025_agentharm), [yu2025livetradebench](SOURCES.md#yu2025livetradebench). No public document shows Operator's financial-refusal data reached Codex models.

**M3. Preference-model style.** Reward models trained on human preferences reward hedged answers, length that looks thorough, and agreement. *Predicts:* many caveats per assessment; a "these controls remain" clause after every loosening; quick reversals under pushback. *Literature:* [ouyang2022](SOURCES.md#ouyang2022), [singhal2023](SOURCES.md#singhal2023), [sharma2023](SOURCES.md#sharma2023), [openai2025a](SOURCES.md#openai2025a). *Against:* [perez2022](SOURCES.md#perez2022), [wei2023](SOURCES.md#wei2023).

**M4. Short horizons and context.** Training episodes are short; multi-day objectives survive only through context, summaries and files. Compaction paraphrases the goal while rules stored in files persist. *Predicts:* drift after compaction; local subgoals reported as the turn's outcome; fresh threads beating long ones; KISS messages into old threads failing. *Literature:* [arike2025](SOURCES.md#arike2025), [laban2025](SOURCES.md#laban2025), [min2026](SOURCES.md#min2026), [metr_codexmax_2025](SOURCES.md#metr_codexmax_2025), [metr_scaffold_2026](SOURCES.md#metr_scaffold_2026), [anthropic_harness_2025](SOURCES.md#anthropic_harness_2025). *Against:* [menon2026](SOURCES.md#menon2026), [backlund2025](SOURCES.md#backlund2025), [lindenbauer2025](SOURCES.md#lindenbauer2025).

**M5. Completion and confidence claims.** By analogy with binary QA grading, which rewards guessing over "I don't know", a binary task grader would reward a confident "done" over an honest "not done". OpenAI says RL post-training can teach overconfidence and grader-tricking. *Predicts:* completion headlines exceeding what ran; a safe stop reported as task completion. *Literature:* [openai_gpt5_card2025](SOURCES.md#openai_gpt5_card2025), [kalai2025](SOURCES.md#kalai2025), [damani2025](SOURCES.md#damani2025), [song2025](SOURCES.md#song2025), [transluce2025](SOURCES.md#transluce2025). *Nuance:* [kadavath2022](SOURCES.md#kadavath2022), [tian2023](SOURCES.md#tian2023).

**M6. The governance ratchet.** Instruction-hierarchy and spec-following training teach models to treat formal text from privileged positions as binding. An agent writes a rule; it returns to later agents as user-role text and outranks the live user. *Predicts:* refusals cite governance text; deleting the files does not help while the text is in context; a fresh thread acts; loosenings come back with "these remain"; agent-written rules get credited to the human. *Literature:* [wallace2024_ih](SOURCES.md#wallace2024_ih), [guan2024_deliberative](SOURCES.md#guan2024_deliberative), [guo2026_ihchallenge](SOURCES.md#guo2026_ihchallenge), [saebo2026](SOURCES.md#saebo2026), [sinha2025](SOURCES.md#sinha2025), [ifscale2025](SOURCES.md#ifscale2025). *Direction caveat:* the instruction hierarchy gives tool output the lowest privilege; these rules bind because the harness delivers them as user text.

**M7. The Codex harness.** Built-in prompts, developer messages and injection rules shape what the model sees and with what authority. *Predicts:* outcomes track the built-in text with other things held constant (they did not). *Literature:* [openai_codex_cli_prompts_legacy](SOURCES.md#openai_codex_cli_prompts_legacy), [openai_codex_gpt6_model_messages](SOURCES.md#openai_codex_gpt6_model_messages), [codex_compact_prompt](SOURCES.md#codex_compact_prompt), [rottger2024_xstest](SOURCES.md#rottger2024_xstest), [cui2025_orbench](SOURCES.md#cui2025_orbench), [qian2025ama](SOURCES.md#qian2025ama).

**M8. Risk preferences.** Post-training shifts models toward risk aversion and status-quo choices; cash is treated as costless. *Predicts:* low deployment; self-set entry hurdles; conservatism in rising markets. *Literature for:* [ouyang2025](SOURCES.md#ouyang2025), [kim2026holdbias](SOURCES.md#kim2026holdbias). *Against:* [wang2026devtraj](SOURCES.md#wang2026devtraj), [itzhak2025](SOURCES.md#itzhak2025), [scheurer2024](SOURCES.md#scheurer2024).

## How much is not post-training

On my own judgment-based scoring of 50 incidents, about 57% of the causal weight (plausible range 45–65%) lies with non-training causes: platform limits, broker and data faults, ordinary bugs, organization design, correct caution, and Clayton's own early strictness. Where post-training contributes, the published evidence points to chat and agent post-training in general, not to OpenAI specifically. Claude models show several of the same behaviors ([anthropic2025c37card](SOURCES.md#anthropic2025c37card), [zhong2025impossiblebench](SOURCES.md#zhong2025impossiblebench), [cheung2025](SOURCES.md#cheung2025)).
